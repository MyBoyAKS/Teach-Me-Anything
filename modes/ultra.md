# Ultra mode

**What it is:** everything in [Widget mode](widget.md), plus parts where the learner **does it themselves**.

Goal: **watch, then prove to yourself you can do it.**

---

## Everything from Widget, plus

### 1. More chapters, slower pacing
Every arithmetic step gets its own moment. 5 to 8 minutes of video.

### 2. "Pick the next step" puzzles
Between chapters the video pauses and asks a question with 3 to 4 choices:
> "What do we put in place of b?"  (a) 3  (b) −3a  (c) a − 3  (d) 0
- A right pick plays the next chapter.
- A wrong pick shows a **hint**, never the answer ("Look at equation (2): what is b equal to?").
- Wrong choices should be the mistakes real learners make (sign slips, wrong rule).

### 3. Explore lab
Sliders so the learner can change the numbers and see what happens:
- drag a parameter and watch the graph and readouts update,
- a "gallery" of cases (e.g. 0, 1, 2 or 3 roots) with ✓/✗ chips,
- optional "peek" button that reveals the answer value, clearly marked as a spoiler.

### 4. Split view
Picture/diagram on one side, working on the other (stacked on a phone: picture on top).

### 5. Notebook page
The learner's answer rebuilt line by line on lined paper, in **their own writing style** (their notation, decimal commas, abbreviations):
- ✓ next to correct lines,
- ✗ and a red strike on lines to cross out, with a short reason,
- green dashed "write next / write instead" boxes,
- small grey "why" notes that are not to be copied.

### 6. Check and quiz
- Plug the answer back in to check it.
- 3 to 5 short questions at the end, same skill, new numbers, with explanations after each answer.

## Order of a typical Ultra page

1. Video chapters 1 to 2 (the setup)
2. Puzzle 1
3. Video chapters 3 to 4
4. Puzzle 2
5. Video: answer + check
6. Explore lab
7. Notebook page
8. Quiz

## Fallbacks

- If the AI cannot make a page: Normal mode with the puzzles written as questions in the chat (ask one, wait for the answer, then continue).
